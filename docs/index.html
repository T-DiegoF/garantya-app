Contract States
Created
Contract deployed, awaiting tenant deposit.
Funded
Deposit locked. Landlord can propose distribution.
DistributionProposed
Proposal sent. Tenant has 7 days to accept or reject.
Disputed
Tenant rejected. Arbitrator has 30 days to resolve.
Completed
Funds allocated. Both parties can withdraw.
Cancelled
Contract cancelled before funding.
